---
title: Is PhotoAdobado free?
description: PhotoAdobado is free and open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 license, and works offline.
order: 10
---

Yes. PhotoAdobado is free and open source under MIT or Apache-2.0, your choice. Its source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/smouj/PhotoAdobado).

PhotoCraft works offline. Its smart selection tools run on your computer, with no cloud and no account.

The upstream ArtCraft trademarks are not included. The Adobado app icon and brand assets have their own attribution in the repository.

See [Install PhotoAdobado](../getting-started/install.md) for current build and download status.
